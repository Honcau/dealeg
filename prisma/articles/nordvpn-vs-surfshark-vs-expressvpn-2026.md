NordVPN, Surfshark, and ExpressVPN are the three most recommended VPNs in 2026, and they're genuinely close in quality — which makes choosing between them confusing. All three offer audited no-logs policies, fast modern protocols, and reliable streaming access. The real differences come down to price, device limits, speed, and specific features. This head-to-head comparison breaks down exactly how they differ so you can pick the right one without overpaying.

## The quick verdict

If you want the short version: NordVPN is the best all-rounder with the largest feature set, Surfshark is the best value with unlimited devices at the lowest price, and ExpressVPN is the most polished and beginner-friendly. All three are excellent — you won't go wrong with any of them — but the right choice depends on what you prioritize. The rest of this guide explains the differences that matter.

## Speed

All three use fast modern protocols, but there are measurable differences. NordVPN's NordLynx protocol (built on WireGuard) consistently ranks among the fastest in independent testing, with minimal speed loss even on distant servers. Surfshark, sharing infrastructure lineage with NordVPN, is similarly fast and also uses WireGuard. ExpressVPN's Lightway protocol is fast and exceptionally stable, though it typically measures slightly behind the other two in raw throughput tests.

For everyday browsing, streaming, and downloads, all three are fast enough that you won't notice the VPN. If you regularly transfer large files or need the absolute fastest connection, NordVPN has a slight edge, but the gap is small.

## Price and device limits

This is where the differences become significant. Surfshark is the clear value winner: its two-year plans are the cheapest of the three, and crucially it allows **unlimited simultaneous devices** on one account. If you have many devices or want to cover your whole family, this alone can make Surfshark the obvious choice.

NordVPN sits in the middle on price, typically allowing up to 10 simultaneous connections. ExpressVPN is the most expensive of the three and historically limited to 8 devices. You're paying a premium for ExpressVPN's polish and track record, which some users value and others find hard to justify given the strong alternatives.

For a family or anyone with more than a handful of devices, Surfshark's unlimited connections plus lowest price is compelling. For an individual who wants the best overall experience, the device limits on NordVPN and ExpressVPN are rarely a real constraint.

## Features

NordVPN has the deepest feature set. Its Threat Protection blocks malware, trackers, and ads even when the VPN is off. It offers Double VPN (routing through two servers), Onion over VPN, dedicated IP options, and Meshnet for linking devices. For users who want more than basic VPN functionality, NordVPN offers the most.

Surfshark punches above its price on features too, including CleanWeb ad-blocking, MultiHop connections, and a Camouflage mode. It offers remarkable feature depth for the lowest price tier.

ExpressVPN keeps things deliberately simpler, focusing on doing the core VPN experience flawlessly across every platform. Its apps are the most refined and consistent, and it's advanced post-quantum protection in its Lightway protocol. It has fewer bells and whistles but executes the essentials better than anyone.

## Privacy and trust

All three have audited no-logs policies and are based in privacy-friendly jurisdictions. ExpressVPN has a particularly strong track record — its transparency reports and a real-world server seizure that recovered no user data have validated its no-logs claim. NordVPN and Surfshark (same parent company) have also passed multiple independent audits.

On privacy, all three are trustworthy. ExpressVPN's proven track record gives it a slight reputational edge, but there's no meaningful privacy reason to avoid any of them.

## Streaming

All three reliably unblock major streaming services across regions — this is a solved problem for the top VPNs. NordVPN and ExpressVPN have very consistent streaming performance, and Surfshark is nearly as reliable. If streaming access is your primary use, any of the three works well.

## How to choose

Choose **Surfshark** if value matters most or you have many devices — the lowest price plus unlimited connections is unbeatable for families and multi-device users. Choose **NordVPN** if you want the best all-around VPN with the deepest features, strong speed, and a slight edge in extras. Choose **ExpressVPN** if you want the most polished, beginner-friendly experience and value its proven privacy track record above saving money.

Whichever you pick, the buying advice is identical: skip the expensive monthly plan, take the long-term discount, search for a working coupon before checkout, and remember all three offer a 30-day money-back guarantee so you can test risk-free. Given how close they are in quality, price and device limits are the deciding factors for most people — which points toward Surfshark for value or NordVPN for the best overall package.
